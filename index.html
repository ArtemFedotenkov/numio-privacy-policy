NUMIO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 Developer / publisher: FEDOT Contact: archigamesads@gmail.com Application: NUMIO (fedot.numio.game)

1. Scope and developer

NUMIO is a number puzzle intended for players aged 13 and older. Core gameplay works offline. Optional online features use Google Play Games and Google AdMob. FEDOT is responsible for NUMIO; Google processes service data under its own terms and privacy policies. FEDOT does not operate a separate game-account, analytics or cloud-save server. Firebase analytics, crash reporting, push notifications and remote configuration are not integrated in this version. In-app purchases and live advertising are not enabled; the game requests Google test banner and rewarded ads.

2. Data on your device and language

NUMIO stores the ordinary level sequence, generation seed and version, initial and remaining board, tasks, move history, points, hint stock and save timestamps. Daily-challenge data includes the UTC date, seed, board, attempt status and result, and whether the result has been acknowledged. These records let you resume the same puzzle, preserve a single daily attempt, restore the ordinary board and avoid awarding the same reward twice. Audio preferences and a flag recording whether the game has requested a review are also stored locally in the application's private storage. Android application backup is disabled. The system language is used locally to choose bundled English, Ukrainian, Russian, Polish or German game and policy text; unsupported languages use English. This requires no online translation service. Device time in UTC is used for daily challenges and save timestamps; it is not GPS location.

3. Google Play Games, points and achievements

Google Play Games may sign in automatically according to your Google and Play Games settings. Google processes authentication, player identifiers, game profile information and gaming activity to provide sign-in, saved games, leaderboards and achievements. NUMIO submits the current progression's absolute point total: one point for an ordinary victory and five for a daily-challenge victory. Separate progressions are not added together. Google retains the highest submitted score; an older version's level-number record is not automatically reduced when scoring changes to points. Achievement requests correspond to 1, 50, 100, 500, 1,000 and 10,000 ordinary levels completed in one progression. Google retains earned achievements and records independently of local resets. Your gamer name, profile image, scores and achievements may be visible to others according to Google's settings. Offline gameplay remains available without sign-in.

4. Cloud saves

When connected, NUMIO uses Google Play Games Saved Games to upload and retrieve the game state described in section 2, including points, hints and daily-challenge progress, to continue play and recover progress across devices. Data is stored in the private application-data area associated with your Google account. NUMIO does not request access to your other Google Drive files or publish your saved board to other players. Synchronization occurs automatically while connected, including after progress changes and when the app is paused. If device and cloud saves differ, the game asks which to use; it does not add their points or hints together. Network errors do not prevent offline play. Changes to or deletion of local data do not necessarily change Google's saved data. Only gameplay progress is uploaded; audio preferences, the local review-request flag and the system-language selection are not part of the cloud save.

5. Test advertising and reviews

The current build requests Google AdMob test banners and rewarded ads. The player requests a rewarded ad when the hint stock is empty. The stock is refilled to five only when the SDK confirms the earned reward. Test mode does not mean that Google's SDKs process no data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, advertising or device/account identifiers, ad/app interactions and diagnostics such as crashes and performance data for advertising, measurement and analytics, security and fraud prevention. IP addresses may indicate approximate location. NUMIO does not request GPS/location permission and has no separate developer analytics service. Advertising choices depend on Google, device, region and configuration. Use the game's Ad privacy entry if Google's configured consent service makes it available. Before live advertising is enabled, this policy, consent configuration and Google Play disclosures will be reviewed. NUMIO may request a Google Play review once after 15 ordinary victories. Google decides whether to show the form and processes any rating or review; the game is not told whether you submitted one. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local records remain until replaced by continued play, cleared in Android app settings or removed on uninstall. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not necessarily delete Google cloud saves, leaderboard scores or achievements. Manage sign-in, connected access, profile visibility and game data in Google / Play Games settings. Google's retention and deletion rules apply to its processing. Playing while signed in after deleting Google game data may upload progress again. Google instructions: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646 NUMIO does not create a separate FEDOT account. FEDOT cannot directly delete data inside your Google account. Contact archigamesads@gmail.com for privacy requests. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to processing, withdraw consent for future consent-based processing, and complain to a supervisory authority. Handling depends on applicable law and which provider holds the data. Google's own processing is described in its policies.

7. Support and the policy website

If you email FEDOT, your email address and message are used to answer your request and retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Email providers also process correspondence under their policies. You may request deletion of this correspondence at archigamesads@gmail.com. The public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address. The pages contain no advertising or added analytics scripts and offer buttons for all five languages. The in-game policy is bundled, readable offline and automatically follows the game language without a language selector. GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

8. Other information and changes

NUMIO has no chat or user-uploaded content and does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location access. It is not directed at children under 13; contact us if you believe a child's personal data has been processed inappropriately. Providers may process data internationally under their policies and applicable safeguards. This policy will be revised when relevant features or practices change.
