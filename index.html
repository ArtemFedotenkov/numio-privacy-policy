NUMIO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 Developer / publisher: FEDOT Contact: archigamesads@gmail.com Application: NUMIO (fedot.numio.game)

1. About this policy

NUMIO is a number puzzle for players aged 13 and older. Core gameplay works offline. Optional online features use Google Play Games and Google AdMob. This policy describes the current version, which requests Google test banner ads; live advertising and purchases are not enabled. FEDOT does not operate its own game-account, analytics, advertising or cloud-save server.

2. Data stored on your device

NUMIO stores the current level, completed-level count, generation seed and version, initial board, remaining tiles, tasks, move history and save timestamps to resume progress and restart the same puzzle. Audio settings and a flag recording whether the game has requested a review are also stored locally. These files are in the application's private storage. Android application backup is disabled.

3. Google Play Games, leaderboard and achievements

Google Play Games may sign in automatically according to your Google and Play Games settings. Google processes account authentication, player identifiers, game profile information and gaming activity to provide these services. NUMIO uses the signed-in game profile to synchronize progress, submit leaderboard scores, unlock achievements and display Google's leaderboard and achievements screens. The leaderboard score is the highest reached level number in the current progression; separate runs are not added together. Achievement unlock requests correspond to 1, 50, 100, 500, 1,000 and 10,000 completed levels within one progression. Google retains earned achievements and the highest submitted score independently of local resets. Your gamer name, profile image, scores and achievements may be visible to other people according to Google's service and profile settings. Offline gameplay remains available without sign-in.

4. Cloud saves

When signed in, NUMIO uses Google Play Games Saved Games to upload and retrieve your game state: level and completed count, generation seed/version, initial board, remaining tiles, tasks, move history and save timestamps. This supports continued play and recovery across devices. The save is stored in the private application-data area associated with your Google account. NUMIO does not request access to your other Google Drive files and does not publish your saved board to other players. If local and cloud saves differ, NUMIO presents a choice of which progress to continue. Network or cloud errors do not prevent offline play. Cloud synchronization occurs automatically while the service is connected, including after progress changes and when the app is paused.

5. Advertising and Google services

The current build uses Google AdMob test banners. Test mode does not mean that no data is processed by Google's SDKs. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/account or advertising identifiers, ad and application interactions, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance data. Google uses this information for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. NUMIO does not request GPS/location permission and has no separate developer-operated analytics service. Applicable advertising choices depend on Google, your device, region and the configured advertising service. The game's Ad privacy entry appears when Google's configured consent service requires a privacy-options entry. Live advertising remains disabled in this version; this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be reviewed before live ads are enabled. NUMIO may request a Google Play review once after you complete level 15. Google controls whether the review form appears and processes any rating or review. The game is not told whether you submitted a rating. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data is retained until replaced by continued play, cleared through Android app settings, or removed when you uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not necessarily delete Google cloud saves, leaderboard records or achievements. Use your Google Play Games / Google account settings to manage sign-in, profile visibility, connected access and game data. Google's retention and deletion rules apply to data processed by Google. If you continue playing while signed in after deleting Google game data, new progress may be uploaded again. Google instructions for deleting Play Games data: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646 NUMIO does not create a separate FEDOT user account. FEDOT cannot directly delete data inside your Google account. For privacy questions or help with a request, contact archigamesads@gmail.com.

7. Support messages and the policy website

If you email FEDOT, your email address and message are used to handle your request. Email providers also process correspondence under their own policies. FEDOT retains correspondence only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of this correspondence at archigamesads@gmail.com. The public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it may cause GitHub to process technical request information, including your IP address, under GitHub's privacy statement. The policy pages contain no advertising or added analytics scripts. The policy displayed inside NUMIO is bundled with the app and can be read offline. GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

8. Other information and updates

NUMIO has no chat or user-uploaded content and does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Data processed by Google or email/hosting providers is handled under their policies, including any applicable international processing. This policy will be updated when relevant features or data practices change; the effective date above identifies the current revision. The latest public version is available at https://artemfedotenkov.github.io/numio-privacy-policy/ .
