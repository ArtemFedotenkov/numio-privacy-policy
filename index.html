NUMIO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 Developer / publisher: FEDOT Contact: archigamesads@gmail.com Application: NUMIO (fedot.numio.game)

NUMIO is an offline puzzle game. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, usage information or diagnostic reports to FEDOT or third parties. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases or network services. It does not request Internet access.

The board, current game state, completed level count, generation data and audio preferences are stored locally to continue your game, replay the same level and remember your sound settings. They are processed only on your device in the app's private storage. Android app backup is disabled. The app does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. FEDOT has no server copy and cannot access or erase it remotely. To remove it, use Android Settings → Apps → NUMIO → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall NUMIO.

If you voluntarily email archigamesads@gmail.com, FEDOT receives your email address and message to respond to your request. Email providers also process correspondence under their own policies. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the same address.

The app has no user content sharing features and collects no data from children or other users. The Google Play Games and Leaderboard options are currently not configured and do not connect to the network. If network features, advertising or data collection are introduced, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before they are enabled.

Privacy questions and correspondence deletion requests: archigamesads@gmail.com.
